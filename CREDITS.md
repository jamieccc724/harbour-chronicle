# Credits — The Harbour Chronicle

## News sources (public feeds)

Facts and headlines in each edition are taken from public RSS feeds and official open data, including, as available:

- BBC News and BBC Sport
- The Guardian
- NPR
- Al Jazeera
- Hong Kong Free Press
- South China Morning Post — headline and short public summary only; article bodies are not used; images are not copied
- news.gov.hk
- CNA (Channel News Asia)
- The Japan Times
- Deutsche Welle
- France 24
- Hong Kong Observatory open data (harbour glass)

## Living plates

Moving plates are official YouTube videos from news organisations (BBC News, Reuters, The Guardian, Al Jazeera English, Associated Press, DW News, France 24, CNA, Sky News, and others as matched), restyled as typographic impressions. Each plate is captioned and linked to the official watch page.

## Type

Cormorant Garamond, IM Fell English, Newsreader, Cinzel, Oswald, IBM Plex Mono, and Noto Serif TC, served by Google Fonts, under their respective open-font licences.

## Original work

The name *The Harbour Chronicle*, the motto *Ink that holds the tide*, the harbour-egret press-mark, the rag-paper textures, and the typographic design of this leaf were made for this edition. They are not taken from any other newspaper or from any copyrighted fictional press.
